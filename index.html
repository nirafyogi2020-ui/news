Rasuwa flood: 1,458 confirmed dead, 5,285 still missing

  1. What happened

    The Bhote Koshi flood struck Rasuwa on 26 August. Search and recovery continue along the Trishuli and Narayani rivers.

  2. Where it stands

    Nepal Police: 1,458 confirmed dead and 5,285 listed missing in Nepal, as of 14 October 2026, 12:00 pm NPT. Missing does not mean dead.

  3. What to do

    In an emergency call 100 or 112. Give only through the Government of Nepal’s own relief fund.
