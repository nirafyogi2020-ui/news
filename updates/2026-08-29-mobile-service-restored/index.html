Nepal Telecom restores mobile service at 80 of 120 flood-hit sites

Nepal Disaster Update LivePublished 29 August 2026, 6:22 am NPTSuperseded by a later briefing

This briefing has been overtaken

It was published at 29 August 2026, 6:22 am NPT and is kept online as the record of what was confirmed at that hour. Figures in it are no longer current.

Read the latest briefing: होर्मुज जलमार्गमा तनाव चर्कियो, इरानद्वारा तीन तेल ट्याङ्कर अवरुद्ध · Current event summary

Nepal Telecom said Saturday it has restored mobile service at 80 of the 120 towers and sites knocked out by the flood in Rasuwa, Nuwakot and Dhading, The Rising Nepal reports. Technicians were flown in by helicopter to reach some of the most remote sites, including towers in Gosainkunda, Suryagadhi and Bidur municipalities. Service in Dhunche, the Rasuwa district headquarters, came back on Thursday.

Phone lines being down is the main reason families cannot reach relatives in the flood zone. If you are trying to find someone, keep trying the same number rather than assuming coverage in their area is still out.
